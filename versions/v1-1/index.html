HOW TO PLAY

  1. Pick a case at the Case Board in base camp, then drive through the gate to that biome.
  2. Walk with the left stick, look by dragging the right side. The yellow arrow points at your target.
  3. DART shoots where you look and locks on when the crosshair turns green. Darts are limited — restock at the camp Dart Supply.
  4. Chill animals let you walk up. Feisty ones charge if you rush in: tap SNEAK, stay downwind (the 💨 arrow shows where the wind blows; approach so it blows from the animal to you), hide in bushes, or tap CREW so a ranger distracts them. Runners bolt: tap DRONE so a freeze ray slows them, or chase in the truck. Tap DRONE again to fly a recon sweep, then RETURN. Never ram!
  5. When an animal sleeps, bring the truck close and tap LOAD. Drive back to camp and tap UNLOAD inside a sanctuary fence.
  6. In the truck: GAS, BRAKE, HANDbrake, steer with the stick or arrows. SEAT moves you to the back so your crew drives while you dart.
  7. Poachers shout when they spot you. If they catch you they take the truck’s cargo (delivered animals stay safe). Use a FLARE or CREW to scare them off. If they grab an animal, bump their jeep to free it.
  8. Rescue points buy dart and truck upgrades. Play with friends: one drives, the others dart!
  9. Poacher mode (from the title) is a cartoon job: net animals into crates, sneak into a sanctuary and unlock a pen, then sell the crates alive to a shady collector at your hideout van. Rangers and a heat meter are after you. Getting caught drops the crates, not your cash. V launches the drone, N throws a net.
